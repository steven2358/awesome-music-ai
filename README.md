# Awesome Music AI

A curated list of AI tools for music composition, generation, and analysis.

## AI music generators

- [Suno](https://suno.com/) - An AI music generator that creates full songs with vocals from text prompts or lyrics.
- [Udio](https://www.udio.com/) - An AI music generator for creating songs with vocals from text prompts.
- [Flow Music](https://www.flowmusic.app/) - An AI music studio by Google for creating, remixing, and sharing songs by chatting with an AI producer.
- [Sonauto](https://sonauto.ai) - AI music generator with lyrics.
- [Mureka](https://www.mureka.ai/) - An AI music generator for creating songs, lyrics, and instrumentals from text prompts or reference audio.
- [Minimax Audio](https://www.minimax.io/audio/music) - AI music generator.
- [Mubert](https://mubert.com) - AI music generator.
- [Tempolor](https://www.tempolor.com) - AI music generator for royalty-free tracks.
- [Beatoven](https://www.beatoven.ai) - Create original background music.
- [MusicGPT](https://musicgpt.com) - AI music generator.
- [Stable Audio](https://stableaudio.com) - Generative AI for music and sound effects.
- [Eleven Labs Music](https://elevenlabs.io/music) - AI music generator.
- [Soundverse](https://www.soundverse.ai/?tool=ai-song-generator) - AI music generator.
- [Boomy](https://boomy.com/) - An AI music generator for creating songs from a chosen genre or mood and releasing them to streaming services.
- [gabotechs/MusicGPT](https://github.com/gabotechs/MusicGPT) - Generate music based on natural language prompts using LLMs running locally. #opensource
- [AIVA](https://www.aiva.ai/) - Personal AI music generation assistant.
- [YuE](https://map-yue.github.io) - A series of open-source foundation models designed for music generation. [#opensource](https://github.com/multimodal-art-projection/YuE)
- [Lyria 3](https://gemini.google.com/music) - An AI music generator by Google.
- [ACE-Step](https://acemusic.ai/) - An open-source model for generating music locally on Mac, AMD, Intel, and CUDA devices. [#opensource](https://github.com/ace-step/ACE-Step-1.5)
- [RAVE](https://github.com/acids-ircam/RAVE) - A model for real-time neural audio synthesis and timbre transfer. #opensource

## AI music analysis

- [Sonoteller](https://sonoteller.ai/) - Song analysis, lyrics analysis and music analysis.
- [MuScriptor](https://github.com/muscriptor/muscriptor) - A model by Kyutai and Mirelo for transcribing multi-instrument recordings into MIDI and sheet music. #opensource
- [Is This Song AI](https://isthissongai.app/) - A free checker that estimates whether a song was made with an AI music generator such as Suno or Udio, from a streaming link or an uploaded file, with a published accuracy test.

## AI music processors

- [LANDR](https://www.landr.com/) - Samples, plugins, distribution and AI mastering engine.
- [Moises](https://moises.ai/) - An AI app for separating songs into stems, detecting chords, transcribing lyrics, and changing tempo or pitch.

## AI in the DAW

- [MAGDA](https://magda.land/) - A DAW with an AI chat assistant that runs natural-language commands for editing, mixing, and sound design. [#opensource](https://github.com/Conceptual-Machines/magda-core)
- [Dawbrain](https://www.dawbrain.com/) - An AI assistant for Ableton Live that writes MIDI, arranges clips, and sets up devices and the mix in the open session.

## AI vocals

- [ACE Studio](https://acestudio.ai/) - An AI vocal synthesizer that generates singing from MIDI and lyrics, with voice cloning and per-note control.
- [Kits AI](https://www.kits.ai/) - An AI vocal platform for voice conversion with licensed artist voices, custom voice training, and harmony generation.

## Distribution

- [DistroKid](https://distrokid.com/) - Distribution app.
